# OG Orbit

A launcher by OGSmuggy, with a favourites dock, app grid, folders and a live settings preview.

## Download

Get [OG Orbit 0.3.1](https://github.com/OGSmuggy/OG-Orbit/releases/tag/v0.3.1), download the APK and confirm Android's installation prompt. Install over your existing copy to keep your settings.

On a phone or tablet, open OG Orbit from the app drawer. You can keep your current Home app. On a TV, choose Orbit as Home if you want it to be your launcher.

For later updates, open About > Updates > Check for updates. You can install now or download for after a restart; Android asks you to confirm installation.

## Store

The Store has Browse and Installed pages with thumbnail cards and installed badges. Install the modules you want, then open their tiles from the Orbit menu. You can remove and reinstall modules through the Store; their saved customisation stays available for later.

Changes appear in the preview while you edit. **Apply** saves them. **Back** leaves unapplied changes behind. The settings also include clearer on/off switches and a colour picker with presets, hue, saturation, brightness and opacity.

## What's in each module?

### Wallpaper

- Choose from the bundled image collection, import your own images, or use gradient backgrounds with light and dark choices.
- Browse MotionBgs categories and pages inside Live Wallpapers. Select a thumbnail to stream a preview, then Download to keep a local copy in My collection.
- Import a local video, a direct HTTPS video link or a supported MotionBgs page link.
- Preview, apply, turn off or remove saved live wallpapers. Video wallpapers loop silently and fill the background.
- Set a 30, 60 or 144 FPS presentation limit and choose whether playback pauses while another app is open. Actual motion depends on the video, screen and device; these settings don't add extra frames to a video.

### Layout Customiser

- Put the dock at the top, bottom, left or right, or hide it.
- Change its length, thickness, position, tile size, transparency, rounded corners and colour. Reset the dock colour when you want the default back.
- Adjust app tile size, corners, horizontal and vertical spacing, labels and the grid from five to ten apps per row.
- Use free placement to move and resize individual apps and folders in the preview. Turn snapping on for easier alignment, or lock positions when you're happy with them.

### Icon Packs

- Choose Original, Round, Square or Mono styles across your apps, with separate choices for individual apps.
- Import compatible icon-pack ZIPs from storage or an HTTPS link.
- Adjust built-in icon size, card corners, outlines, background colours and outline colours.
- Browse packs and individual apps in thumbnail galleries. Your imported artwork and manual app artwork stay separate from the built-in style controls.

### Screensavers

- Choose a clock, photo slideshow or saved video loop.
- Change clock style, size, position and colour, and show or hide the clock and photo captions where the chosen mode allows it.
- Use bundled wallpapers, your personal images or both. Choose a photo or slideshow and set the change interval from 10 to 120 seconds.
- Preview changes in the settings window or open a full-screen preview before applying them.
- Open Android's screensaver and display settings from the Activation page. Automatic screensaver support and timing depend on your device's Android settings.

### Widgets & Cards

- Add clock, date, weather, calendar and device information cards to Home.
- Choose which cards are visible and adjust their size, position, transparency, headings and rounded or square corners.
- Set text and background colours separately.
- Find weather by city or postcode and choose Celsius or Fahrenheit.
- Add a calendar using an HTTPS ICS calendar feed. This uses the feed you choose rather than signing in to a calendar account.

### Preview Customiser

- Resize, move, show or hide the Home preview window in settings.
- Change rounded corners, border width and frame colour.
- Pick a surrounding background colour or use a saved live wallpaper loop independently of your Home background.
- Reset the window, frame or complete preview to its defaults.

### Discover

- Add movie, TV and anime cards, Android Watch Next cards, or cards published by a compatible installed app.
- Choose the maximum number of cards, move and resize the row, and adjust card size and spacing.
- Reserve space above the apps, use snapping for placement, or lock the row in place.
- Show or hide titles and provider labels, change text sizes, round the corners and adjust transparency.
- Control auto-scroll, how long each card stays, slide/fade/instant transitions, animation speed, easing and reduced motion.
- Online movie, TV and anime cards open supported Stremio links. Watch Next and app cards depend on what compatible apps publish on your device.

## Compatibility and feedback

One APK is used for TV, tablet and phone. Orbit currently uses a landscape layout. The touch fixes were checked on Samsung SM-X216B with Android 16; phone testing is ongoing. Earlier TV testing used Chromecast with Google TV HD on Android 14.

Automatic screensaver activation, published TV cards and available video formats depend on the device and installed apps. For help, open an issue with your device model, Android version and what happened.
